Audit one branch

A network audit tells you how the dealership is doing. A branch audit tells a branch manager what to fix on Monday morning. Upload the same DMS extracts either way — the audit filters every file, including the complaint extracts, to the branch you pick, so a branch report only ever sees its own records.



Scoring… about half a minute.

Include the Physical Audit Sheet exported from the phone in the same upload and pillar J is scored from it. Several branches' sheets can go in together. If none is attached, anything still held from a recent mobile walk is used instead, and the results page says so.

How the two halves meet

Phone The auditor walks the showroom and answers 62 items across 12 sections. Every tap is saved on the device and posted to the server; out of signal, answers queue up and sync by themselves.
Server The captures are held per branch. The latest submitted audit for a branch is the one that scores.
Laptop Upload the DMS extracts and run the branch. The captures are assembled into a Physical Audit Sheet in the ordinary format and scored as pillar J — 10 of the 100 weightage points — with critical failures raising corrective actions automatically.
